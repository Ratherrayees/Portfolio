Privacy Policy for RentEase

Last Updated: June 16, 2026

Rayees Ahmad Rather ("we," "us," or "our") operates the RentEase mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in the App.

1. Information Collection and Use

RentEase is designed as an "offline-first" application.

  • User-Provided Data: You may enter tenant names, phone numbers, email addresses, and financial records into the App.
  • Storage: All data you enter is stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database.
  • No Server Uploads: We do not collect, store, or transmit your data to any external servers. We do not have access to the information you input into the App.

2. Permissions

To provide its features, the App requires certain permissions:

  • Notifications: To send you reminders about lease expirations and overdue payments.
  • Exact Alarms: To ensure time-sensitive reminders are delivered accurately.
  • Storage/Media: To allow you to export PDF invoices or save database backups to your device.
  • Biometrics (Optional): If enabled by you, to secure access to the App using your device's fingerprint or face unlock.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution and updates.
  • Google Fonts: Used for typography within the App.
  • System Sharing: When you share an invoice via WhatsApp, Email, or other apps, that data is handled by the third-party app you choose.

4. Data Security

The security of your data depends on the security of your device. We recommend using device-level encryption and screen locks to protect your information. Since all data is local, if you delete the App or lose your device without a manual backup, the data cannot be recovered by us.

5. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising SDKs. We do not track your behavior or usage patterns.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: rayiesamin@gmail.com
